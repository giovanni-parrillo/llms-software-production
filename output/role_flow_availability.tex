% Availability of the review-role flow outcomes (baseline sample of the main specification).
% Source: prepare_data() on repo_month.csv, p6 sample (repo-months with a 6-month baseline), role_flows.R.
\begin{paragraph}
Role-flow outcomes are defined only for repository-months with at least ten interactions and some human activity in the preceding twelve months. Availability therefore depends on treatment: after adoption, 80.7\% of eventually-treated repository-months have a defined flow, against 32.5\% of eventually-treated repository-months before adoption and 19.5\% of never-treated repository-months. The role-flow estimates thus compare outcomes in a sample whose composition changes with adoption. The baseline activity covariate, measured before adoption, does not address this. The main role-flow estimates use the current sample and are conditional on availability.
\end{paragraph}
